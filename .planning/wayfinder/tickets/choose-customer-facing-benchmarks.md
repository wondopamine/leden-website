## Question

Which shipped café, restaurant, and mobile-ordering experiences should Café Le
Den benchmark for brand trust, food desire, information hierarchy, menu browsing,
item configuration, pickup-slot choice, cart, confirmation, and mobile conversion;
and what specific pattern should be learned from each without copying its visual
identity?

## Resolution comment

Decision: use a focused six-product portfolio rather than one visual reference:
Lune Croissanterie for appetite and first-screen hierarchy; BOSSA Montréal for
local and bilingual trust; Blank Street for the compact coffee-order loop and
error-state lessons; Chipotle for modifier structure; Starbucks Canada for
pickup-slot review and confirmation; and Uber Eats Pickup for cart, status, order
identity, and recovery. Borrow only the named interaction/content patterns;
Café Le Den keeps its own cream/forest/orange identity, real photography,
guest flow, and pay-in-person model. Mobbin's paid MCP is unnecessary; the public
catalog supplied the relevant shipped-screen evidence.
