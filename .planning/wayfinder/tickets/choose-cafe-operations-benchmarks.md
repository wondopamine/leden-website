## Question

Which shipped restaurant admin, order hub, and kitchen-display experiences should
Café Le Den benchmark for live order triage, status progression, sold-out control,
menu editing, service recovery, analytics, and high-volume scanning; and which
patterns are essential for one café rather than enterprise bloat?

## Resolution comment

Resolved on 2026-08-15. Use [Toast Orders Hub, Square KDS/Order Manager and item
availability, Lightspeed KDS 2.0, and lean Square reporting as the complementary
benchmark set](../research/cafe-operations-benchmarks.md). The essential pattern
is a single live, oldest/due-first ticket board with explicit progression,
timers and persistent new-order attention, highly readable modifiers/notes,
undo plus labelled recall, immediate sold-out synchronisation, and honest lean
operational analytics. Reject multi-location, multi-station, delivery, POS
settlement, labour, procurement, hardware, and advanced-BI machinery as
enterprise bloat. Mobbin remains optional visual-polish input and is not needed
to establish the restaurant workflow.
